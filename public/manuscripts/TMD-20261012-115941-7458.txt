this loneliness will consume me  
swallow me as a whole  
until all i am left with is  
burning ashes of myself, existing all alone   
i say i’m fine  
its alright   
until its 11pm and i’m sobbing on the way home  
contemplating on whether i can go back  
and face being all alone   
i find these thoughts hidden in unread books and unheard words   
no matter where i run or conceal   
it keeps lingering around like dust  
half-written poems, long-paused clauses  
eyes filled with transparent tears and misaligned fears  
these overwhelm me to the point where  
i end up unknowingly staring at the stars  
feeling as if my life is something i’m witnessing from afar  
i can’t be the only one going through this  
because it does seem like it  
like the whole world is going on smoothly   
while my days are on freeze
